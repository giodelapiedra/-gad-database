# 🎬 CITY GOVERNMENT OF TANAUAN — GENDER AND DEVELOPMENT
## GAD Portal — Product Showcase Script
### *"The first all-in-one GAD portal. No hassle. Submit in minutes."*

> **Positioning:** The City Government of Tanauan is the **first to build a complete, all-in-one GAD portal** — everything from online forms to approval and reporting, in one place. Proudly built for Tanauan.

**Length:** ~2–3 min · **Tone:** product showcase / launch · **URL:** `http://localhost:5173/templates`

---

## ✅ Before you record (prep)
- Log in as an **encoder** who has an **assigned barangay** (so the location auto-fills on camera).
- Make sure the **sidebar is open** (panel icon, top-left) for clean framing.
- Have at least one existing submission (approved or pending) so the status tracker has something to show.
- Close other tabs; set browser zoom to 100%.

---

## 🎯 HOOK (0:00–0:20)
**On screen:** A quick flash of the old way — a messy Excel sheet with many columns and manual typing. Then **cut** to the clean GAD Portal dashboard.

**Say:**
> "Preparing GAD forms used to be slow — spreadsheets, manual columns, endless re-typing, and back-and-forth corrections.
> **Not anymore.** The **City Government of Tanauan** is proud to be the **first to build a complete, all-in-one GAD Portal** — where submitting is completely **hassle-free**."

---

## ⚡ THE WHOLE PROCESS IN 3 STEPS

### Step 1 — Choose (0:20–0:45)
**On screen:** Scroll to "Create a new form." Hover a card → click **Fill Form Online.**

**Say:**
> "Step one: **pick a form.** Barangay or City, Plan & Budget or Accomplishment Report — just one click."

### Step 2 — Fill in (0:45–1:30) ⭐
**On screen:** Point to the **Region / Province / City / Barangay fields — already filled and locked.** Fill one or two rows. Show the auto-computed sub-total and the **GAD Budget Share** indicator.

**Say:**
> "Step two: **fill it in.** And notice — the **Region, Province, City, and Barangay are already set.** Nothing to type. The system already knows where you are.
> Just enter the content — and the **totals compute automatically**, while it even checks that you meet the required **5% GAD budget.** No formulas, no errors."

### Step 3 — Submit (1:30–1:50) ⭐
**On screen:** Click **Submit for Approval.** Show the success state and the new "Pending" entry in the status tracker.

**Say:**
> "Step three: **submit.** One click — done. It goes straight to the admin for review. **That fast.**"

---

## 🔁 ALREADY HAVE AN EXCEL FILE? (1:50–2:25) ⭐
**On screen:** On `/templates`: click **Download blank Excel** (show that the file already has the location filled in) → then **Upload filled Excel** → the form opens **already populated.**

**Say:**
> "Prefer working in Excel? No problem. **Download the blank template** — the location is already filled in — complete it offline, then **upload it.**
> The system **automatically recognizes** the form and **fills in everything** for you. Just review and submit. **Zero re-typing.**"

---

## 📊 TRACK IT, NO GUESSWORK (2:25–2:45)
**On screen:** Status tracker / My Submissions — the stepper: Submitted → Reviewed → Approved. Point to the notification bell.

**Say:**
> "And you're never left guessing — you can **see the status** at every step, and you get a **notification** when it's approved or sent back for corrections. Clear, always up to date."

**On screen:** Quick montage across the sidebar — forms, submissions, resources, trainings, records, reports.

**Say:**

> online forms, Excel in and out, approvals, status tracking, notifications, GAD resources, trainings, beneficiary records, reports, and HGDG checklists.
> No more scattered files. No more separate tools. **One portal for everything.**"

**All-in-one, in one place:**

- 📥 Excel in & out — download blank, upload to auto-fill
- ✅ Approval & return workflow

## 🎙️ Showcase tips
- Keep the pace **energetic but clear**; pause briefly on every "auto" moment (auto-location, auto-compute, auto-fill) — that's the wow.
- Three recurring beats: **"Nothing to type" · "Automatic" · "One click."**
- Add on-screen callouts: *"Auto-filled location"*, *"Auto-computed totals"*, *"Auto-detect & fill"*, *"One-click submit."*
- For a 60-second cut: **Hook → 3 Steps → Payoff** only.

---

### What's being showcased (new features)
1. Redesigned, premium encoder dashboard (`/templates`)
2. **Fill Form Online** with auto-filled, locked location (Region/Province/City/Barangay)
3. Auto-computed sub-totals + 5% GAD budget share check
4. **Download blank Excel** with location pre-filled (offline workflow)
5. **Upload filled Excel** → auto-detect form type → auto-fill the online form
6. One-click **Submit for Approval**
7. Status tracking (Submitted → Reviewed → Approved) + notifications
8. Built-in guided **Demo** tour
